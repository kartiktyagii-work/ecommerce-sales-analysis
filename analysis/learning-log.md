# Learning log

One line every time you get stuck, use a hint, or change your mind. Takes ten seconds; by Day 5
it is your revision notes, your interview prep, and the honest answer to *"what was hard about
this project?"*

Do not curate this. Messy and honest beats tidy and retrospective.
