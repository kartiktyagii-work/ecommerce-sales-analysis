# Problem statement

## 1. Business background

An e-commerce company operates across multiple regions and sells a wide range of products through
its online platform. The company generates a large volume of transactional data containing
information about customers, orders, products, payments and returns.

Although the company has access to this data, management currently lacks a centralised analytical
view of sales performance, customer behaviour, product profitability and regional trends. As a
result, business teams find it difficult to identify the key factors driving revenue and
profitability, understand customer purchasing patterns, and determine areas requiring improvement.

## 2. Business problem

The company needs a data-driven solution providing management with clear insight into overall sales
and revenue performance, profitability across products and categories, customer purchasing behaviour
and retention, regional and geographic performance, product demand, order and return trends, and
changes in performance over time.

The primary challenge is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights
supporting decisions on **sales strategy, customer retention, product management and regional
growth**.

## 3. Objective

Develop an analytical solution combining **SQL, data analysis and Power BI** that lets stakeholders:

1. Monitor key sales and profitability KPIs.
2. Identify high- and under-performing products and categories.
3. Analyse customer purchasing patterns and identify valuable segments.
4. Measure customer retention and repeat-purchase behaviour.
5. Evaluate regional and geographic sales performance.
6. Analyse returns and their impact on revenue and profitability.
7. Identify trends and changes in performance over time.
8. Provide actionable recommendations to improve revenue, profitability and retention.

An interactive Power BI dashboard backed by SQL analysis and a structured data model, letting
stakeholders drill from overall KPIs into products, customers and regions — and answering three
management questions: **what is happening**, **why is it happening**, and **what should the business
do about it**.
